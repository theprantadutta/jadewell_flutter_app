# Refund Policy for Jadewell: Mahjong Solitaire

**Policy Version: 1.0**  
**Effective Date: November 18, 2026**  
**Last Updated: November 18, 2026**

All purchases in Jadewell are made through **Google Play** or the **Apple App Store**. The store takes the payment, so refunds are issued by the store under its own policy. We are glad to help if something went wrong.

## What you can buy

- **Remove ads:** a one-time purchase. Jadewell has no subscriptions.

## Requesting a refund

**Google Play (Android)**  
- Within 48 hours of purchase, you can usually request a refund yourself at https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory or through the Google Play Help Center.  
- After 48 hours, email us (below) with your order ID (it starts with `GPA.`) and we will review your request.

**Apple App Store (iOS)**  
- Request a refund at https://reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple decides on App Store refunds; we cannot issue them directly.

## If a purchase did not arrive

If you were charged but ads didn't turn off, open the **Remove ads** screen and tap **Restore** first. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with your order ID and we will fix it or support a refund.

If a refund is granted, Remove ads is turned off again.

## Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the consumer laws of your country, such as the EU and UK right of withdrawal where it applies to digital content.
