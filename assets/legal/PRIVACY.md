# Privacy Policy for Jadewell: Mahjong Solitaire

**Legal Version: 1.0**  
**Policy Version: 1.0**  
**Effective Date: November 18, 2026**  
**Last Updated: November 18, 2026**

Jadewell ("we," "our," or "us") is a mahjong solitaire game made by Pranta Dutta, an independent developer based in Bangladesh. This policy explains what Jadewell collects, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and how to delete it.

The short version: you can play Jadewell without giving us your name or email. It starts you as a guest with a random ID, keeps your progress on your device, and sends your results to our server for the daily leaderboard and friends. Signing in with Google, Apple or an email address is optional. Google services handle analytics, crash reports and ads. You can delete your account at any time in the app.

## 1. What we collect

### Information stored only on your device

Your settings, layouts cleared, best times and stars, the board you are playing, your daily results and streak, and a list of results waiting to be sent to our server are stored on your device.

### Information sent to our server

- **Guest ID.** On first use, Jadewell signs in with Firebase Anonymous Authentication, which creates a random identifier. Unless you choose to sign in (below), it is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any other account.
- **Account (optional).** If you sign in with Google, Apple or an email address and password, we receive your email address and which sign-in you used. With Google or Apple, the provider shares your email address and basic profile with Firebase; we store only the email address, never your real name or photo. With email and password, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and never reaches our server.
- **Profile.** A generated display name and username (for example "SunnyOrchid4558"), an avatar colour and a friend code. You can change your display name, username and colour in the app. Please don't use your real name.
- **Game results.** For each board you finish or leave: the layout, time, stars, how many hints, shuffles and undos you used, tiles matched and longest streak.
- **Progress.** Your best time and stars per layout, totals (games played, boards cleared, tiles matched, time played), your daily streak, and which layouts you unlocked (including by watching a video).
- **Daily results.** Your time and stars on each daily board, with the list of moves you made and when you made them. We use the moves only to check that the result is a real clear before it is ranked.
- **Friends.** The friend requests you send, receive, accept or decline.
- **Timezone offset.** The number of minutes between your local time and UTC (for example, +360), used to work out which day's daily board is yours. It is not your location.
- **Purchase records.** If you buy Remove ads, the store's receipt or purchase token, order ID and the store's verification response, which we check with Google Play or the Apple App Store, and the resulting entitlement.
- **App details.** Each request includes a random install ID, the app version and the platform (Android or iOS), so we can tell builds apart when fixing problems. Our server does not store them.

### Information collected by Google services in the app

- **Analytics (Firebase Analytics).** In the released app only: anonymous events about how the game is played, for example a board started, cleared or left, a hint or shuffle used, a daily board completed, a setting changed, or an ad or purchase screen shown, with details like the layout and time taken. We strip identifiers such as names, emails and friend codes from these events, and we do not link them to your account. Firebase also collects standard device information (model, OS version, app version, language and an app-instance ID) and, on Android, may collect the advertising ID.
- **Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).** In the released app only, when the app crashes or hits an error: the stack trace, device model, OS version, app version and an installation ID.
- **Advertising (Google AdMob).** To show ads, AdMob processes your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information. With your consent (where consent is required), ads may be personalized; otherwise they are non-personalized.

### What we do not collect

We do not collect your real name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment card details. We collect your email address only if you choose to sign in. Jadewell does not send push notifications. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

## 2. How we use it

- To run the game's online features: the daily, weekly and friends leaderboards, friend requests, and checking purchases.
- To check daily results by replaying your moves, so the leaderboard stays fair.
- To send account emails you ask for, such as resetting your password. We do not send marketing email.
- To understand which layouts are too hard or too easy and to fix problems (analytics and crash reports).
- To show ads that keep the game free, and to respect a Remove ads purchase.
- To prevent cheating and abuse of leaderboards and purchases, and to answer your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. What other players can see

- **Leaderboards** show your display name, avatar colour, stars and time to all players.
- **Friends**, people you send a request to, and anyone who enters your friend code also see your username and friend code.
- Your email address is never shown to other players.

## 4. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

- **Contract:** online features you choose to use, such as leaderboards, friends, signing in and purchases.
- **Legitimate interests:** keeping the game working and improving it (analytics and crash reports), checking daily results, and preventing cheating and fraud. You can object by emailing us.
- **Consent:** personalized ads, and on iOS the App Tracking Transparency permission. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings > Ad privacy choices** (shown where this applies).

## 5. Your ad and tracking choices

- In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Jadewell shows Google's consent form before any ad. Where required, the form also offers US state privacy choices, such as opting out of the sale or sharing of personal information. No ads are requested until consent allows it.
- On iOS, Jadewell asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before the first ad. If you decline, ads are not personalized using the advertising identifier.
- You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's settings.
- Ads are limited to an audience rating of PG.
- Buying **Remove ads** turns off the ads that appear on their own (after a cleared board, and when you return to the app). Videos you choose to watch for a reward stay available.

## 9. Deleting your data

- **In the app:** open **Settings > Account > Delete account**. This permanently deletes your server account and everything linked to it (profile, email address, results, progress, daily results, friends and friend requests, purchase records and your sign-in), removes you from the leaderboards, and clears the progress stored on your device. Your Remove ads purchase belongs to your Google Play or Apple account, so you can restore it afterwards with **Restore** on the Remove ads screen.
- **Signing out** keeps your account; deleting it removes it.
- **Reset progress** (Settings) clears your progress on the device and your best times, stars, unlocks and totals on our server, but keeps your account, daily results and game history.
- **By email:** if you can't use the app, write to prantadutta1997@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, or include your username or friend code. We will delete your account and reply within 30 days.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR and California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 11. Children

Jadewell is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. Security

Data sent between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to the server and to the admin tools is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.
